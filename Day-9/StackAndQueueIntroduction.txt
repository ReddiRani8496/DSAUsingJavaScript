Queue:
-----
-> FIFO First In First Out
methods:
-------
1) enqueue(add)
2) dequeue(remove)
3) peek/front

Examples: Ticket Counter, Os Track Scheduling, Printers, Queue

Stack:
-----

LIFO - Last In First Out
methods:
1) push
2) pop
3) peek/top

Examples: Stack of books, undo feature, browsing history, recursion

Why do we need them?
-------------------
1) Organize data logically
2) Order of operations matter
3) optimize time/space for specific use cases

Use Cases:
----------
1) recursion - Stack
2) Level order tree traversal - Queue
3) BFS - Queue
4) DFS - Stack
